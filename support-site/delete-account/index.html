Request account deletion

Use this page if you can no longer access DebtFree. A verified request removes the account and its associated app data, subject to the retention exceptions described in the Privacy Policy.

How to request deletion

  1. Send your request from the email address associated with your DebtFree sign-in.
  2. Include “DebtFree account deletion” in the subject. Do not include passwords, access tokens, or full financial-account details.
  3. The support team will verify the request and confirm when deletion is complete.
