Help & guides

Make a clear plan for your debt.

DebtFree helps you organize the balances, bills, and payments you enter, then shows payoff estimates based on those details.

Manual tracking: DebtFree does not connect to your bank or import bank transactions. Add and update information yourself so you can review what the app is using.

Getting started

Set up your household

Choose your currency and enter household settings in Settings. These values help the app present your plan; estimates depend on the information you provide.

Add debt accounts

Enter each account's name, balance, APR, minimum payment, and other available terms. Review account details when lender statements change.

Record activity

Add transactions and recurring expenses in the matching sections. A recorded entry represents information you entered; it does not verify a payment with a lender.

Understand your plan

Snowball puts the smallest eligible balance first. Avalanche puts the highest APR first. Payoff dates and interest projections are estimates calculated from the account terms and payments you enter. They are not lender quotes or a promise of a payoff date.

Progress reports summarize recorded activity and entered balance references. They may be incomplete when you began tracking after an account was opened or when older transactions were not entered.

Account and data help

Update your household and account details from Settings. For an account deletion, open Settings → Data & security → Delete account in a build where account deletion is enabled.
