# Required fields source audit - 2026-11-08

Manager integration completed the separate HomeScreen debt editor: account category, debt name, current balance, APR and minimum payment now carry required markers; editable numeric/name inputs have Required accessibility hints. Budget's Due day marker was checked and corrected on Android. Required category selectors also carry markers even when an existing valid default satisfies them. No validation rules were added or removed.

This is a source audit, not runtime acceptance. Markers reflect existing validation; defaults and conditional visibility remain unchanged. The UI input/validation references below were inspected in source, including a search across TSX files. Backend validation and every runtime path were not exercised.

- Sign-in/create account: provider buttons, no email/password fields in the native form.
- Signup: at least one goal; referral source; prior consideration answer when AI is selected (selection currently defaults to no); partner and professional answers. See validateSignUpStep2?4 in src/onboarding-domain.ts and the signup screens in src/OnboardingAndHome.tsx.
- Onboarding income: primary take-home amount greater than zero; additional source name and positive amount when adding one. Frequency has a default. Onboarding debt editor: name, valid current balance, APR, minimum payment; monthly due day for mortgage/auto during initial onboarding. Original balance, credit limit and otherwise due day are optional. See validateIncomeStep, validateDebtDraft and save handler in src/OnboardingAndHome.tsx. Extra payment accepts blank as zero.
- Debt: statement balance is required when reconciling. Strategy extra payment accepts blank as zero; linking recurring payments is optional. Inline editor presented by HomeScreen uses HomeScreen's save validation: name, balance, APR and minimum payment required; due day and mortgage escrow optional but validated if entered. HomeScreen is outside T094 ownership, so its labels were not edited and need coordination.
- Transactions: amount and date required on add/edit; debt account required for debt categories. Category is defaulted. Loan principal, interest, escrow and fees are optional; values supplied are parsed and subject to ledger allocation validation. Payee, note and payment-cycle selection are optional. See handleSaveTransaction and handleSaveEdit in src/screens/TransactionsScreen.tsx and ledger validation.
- Settings: extra monthly payment accepts blank as zero; backup JSON is an action-specific input, not a financial edit field. No required financial field marker needed there.
- Budget (T092 owns): income source name and positive amount; recurring bill name, positive amount, due day and category; custom category name only for Other, credit account only for credit payment. See saveIncome and handleSaveBill in src/screens/BudgetScreen.tsx. Concurrent T092 edits may change this snapshot; coordinate its final labels with that worker.
- Home and Progress were inspected read-only; Home hosts the separate debt editor noted above. No required input was identified in Progress. Upcoming payment actions and scheduled filters do not introduce a required editable form field.

Accessibility hints were added to editable required inputs where practical. Choice-group headings carry markers; individual choices retain their existing accessibility labels. SelectDropdown receives the marker at the debt-account call sites only. No tests, typecheck, build, device, live data or backend writes were performed.
