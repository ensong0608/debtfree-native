## Mandatory interactions

1. **Behavioral model and mobile refinement:** the local references prescribe mechanics, not a visual clone (references/PROTOTYPE_INTERACTION_PATTERNS.md:10; references/prototype/README.md:7). Native screen layouts exist; visual quality and mobile accessibility require device review, not source certification.
2. **Home upcoming card:** future items use a 14-day window and exclude confirmed paid states (src/screens/HomeScreen.tsx:173, :221, :235, :276, :289); the card is conditional and links to Budget Upcoming (:966, :974). Past-due inclusion (:177, :186, :309) is an approved extension recorded in TRACKER.md:143 and :45, not an open product question. Pending confirmation remains visible; verify that pending, partial and confirmed settlement states suppress or retain reminders correctly against the accepted behavior (TRACKER.md:276). Budget Upcoming enumerates current/next cycles for bills and current-cycle debt minimums (src/screens/BudgetScreen.tsx:894, :993), so “full list” should be verified against the intended horizon, including next-month debt minimums.
3. **Home category cycling:** The confirmed decision requires Cards & Loans, Auto and All Debts and excludes Mortgage from Home category views (APP_PLAN.md:147). Instead, SnapshotCard renders Cards & Loans, Home and Auto segments (src/components/FinancialScreen.tsx:157, :171, :175), omitting All Debts. HomeScreen passes the stored debt list directly (:827); categoryDebts returns Mortgage records for Home and all records for All Debts (src/onboarding-domain.ts:114–119). Thus stored mortgages can appear in the Home snapshot; the All Debts helper would also include them if exposed. Correct the category choices and exclusion while retaining the required category cycling concept and mobile refinement.
4. **Strategy redirect:** See all routes to Budget Strategy (src/screens/HomeScreen.tsx:753, :831, :835; src/FiveTabShell.tsx:44, :51, :54; src/screens/HomeScreen.tsx:716–719). The enclosing strategy Card is a plain Card (:828), with no card-body navigation; only its child controls respond. Add an accessible non-conflicting card-body target and verify both routes, while retaining strategy controls.
5. **Debt detail popup:** account press sets selectedId (src/screens/DebtScreen.tsx:403), then renders a replacement FinancialScreen with a back button (:213, :215), not a popup. Tabs are Overview, Transactions, Manage / Terms (:12), not the specified Detail. Implement the requested three-tab modal/sheet and return/dismiss behavior, preserving edit, delete and reconciliation.
6. **Strategy schedule:** account row toggles expanded state and renders Month, Payment, Interest, Balance with unavailable state (src/screens/BudgetScreen.tsx:1350, :1357, :1377, :1389). Source wiring meets the stated shape; verify open/close and projection values at runtime with controlled examples.
