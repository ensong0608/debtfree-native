# Whole-app premium proposal

2026-11-08. Design-only review requested by the owner after the first Debt/Progress implementation. **No application implementation is approved by this document.**

## Assessment

The current app is cleaner but still lacks a consistent premium composition. Debt has too much accumulated padding between the balance, account controls and groups. The Light mapping makes Home and forms feel like a sequence of pale generic boxes. Screens also repeat similarly weighted cards, leaving important amounts insufficiently distinct.

The proposed direction uses one focal financial element per page, tighter section rhythm, warm neutral Light canvas with white surfaces and dark forest numerical panels, and layered charcoal/forest Dark surfaces. Existing category identities remain consistent between bar, icon and amount. Light uses darker counterparts of those hues for legibility. Typography, dividers, icon badges, corner shapes and interaction framing form a shared system rather than a unique treatment on every page.

## Evidence and boundaries

Before panels are **simplified source-based reconstructions**, not pixel-exact screenshots of every current page. The same synthetic names and amounts are used on both sides for a layout comparison. Original native captures are available in the artifact's expandable reference area; the Light Home capture predates the latest wording and is labeled accordingly. The native app was not changed or recaptured for this design review.

The main synthetic case reconciles: Cards & Loans 10,950 + Auto 16,500 = total debt 27,450; income 6,400 minus category commitments 5,580 = remaining 820. Budget Debt payments 1,240 is card minimums 590 + extra 650; the Auto payment 690 appears only under Transportation. Utilization is 10,950 / 25,000 = 43.8%, with a 7,500 target at 30%. Confirmed payment 450 + Auto cashflow payment 690 = 1,140, while unallocated Auto principal does not reduce the balance. The ledger's 42 interest + 15 subscription - 450 card payment gives net debt change -393; checking groceries do not increase debt. Mortgage is explicitly a different illustrative scenario.

Primary sheet actions have reserved bottom space rather than being clipped beneath the phone edge. The new recurring-bill sample has no selected category and therefore shows its save action disabled. This is a design behavior proposal; existing validation must remain authoritative. Form fields still scroll independently, including conditional fields not shown in the representative state.

Decorative orbit linework is not a financial chart. Rings show a supported snapshot percentage; no historical trend, payoff event or award is invented. Only recorded first payment is earned in the milestone example. The utilization goal remains current state; payoff milestones remain locked.

Existing tab order/orientation, Home redirects, chronological grouping, income editing parity, radio default, required-field validation and payment/recovery safeguards remain requirements. Snapshot reduction is not payment history. The mock controls do not execute financial actions.
