Option A • Rounded Outline

Lightest visual footprint and easiest to expand, but less immediate active/inactive contrast at a quick peripheral glance.

Best balance for a modern fintech tool: layered depth separates overlapping concepts (stacked debt cards, area progress) while staying calm at 24px.
