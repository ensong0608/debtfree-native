Component-by-Component Visual Audit & Rationale
Canvas & Surfaces
Flat Charcoal Stack: Background (#1F201F) and cards (#272D2A) have near-identical lightness. Heavy 1px border (#37413B) repeats on every box, creating relentless "card fatigue".
Elevated Depth: Deep canvas (#0C0E0D) with warm tinted surface (#141816) and radial gradient hero (#1A2621). Subtle hairline borders (#222A25) with top specular glow.
Hero & Primary KPI
Fragmented Greeting: User greeting sits alone above an isolated "Estimated Date" card. Date shares same container styling as regular lists with no emotional resonance.
Single Anchor Hero: Merges greeting + projected payoff date into an emotional anchor (32px typography) with context pill ("14 Months Faster"). Instant user motivation.
Debt Snapshot
Competing Dropdown: Heavy dark select box sits above ring gauge. Small label "snapshot" lacks meaning. "Balance change" fails to clarify principal reduction.
Integrated Segmented Pill: Compact category switcher embedded in header. Dual metric hierarchy shows remaining debt vs paid principal with smooth SVG gradient track.
Color Semantics
Teal Saturation: Brand teal (#81DFD2) is applied to badges, links, selected pills, values, and buttons, losing all visual contrast and semantic value.
Restrained Semantic Palette: Mint (#76DCB9) reserved for brand hero & key actions; Soft Green (#86E3B8) for debt reduction; Coral (#F08B78) for charges; Amber (#F5C563) for pending.
Alerts & Badges
Badge Clutter: Outlined boxes (#1, Pending, Past Due) beside account names create visual noise and repeat subtitle text.
Quiet Indicators: Numbered circular discs for ranking, subdued amber pills for pending states, and single-line status subtitles without redundant labels.
