What changed in this round

Dark surfaces are neutral graphite, slate, ink or warm charcoal rather than all green. Brand color is reserved for focal panels, controls and identity. The income editor loses its decorative emblem and large conversion card: it becomes a content-sized bottom sheet with 44px fields, concise labels, a one-line monthly equivalent and a reserved save action. Other pages adopt a tighter spacing rhythm without reducing required information.

Balance comparison

Reference is the sum of the higher of current and entered starting balance for each account. It is not recorded payment history.
